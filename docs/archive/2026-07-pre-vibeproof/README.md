# Pre-VibeProof Documentation Archive

This archive preserves product research and design exploration that existed before the documentation was consolidated around VibeProof on 15 July 2026.

## Status

These files are historical sources, not canonical product guidance. They include earlier KerjaProof branding, Unity and 3D feasibility work, raw Miro material, broad serious-game concepts, and the debates that led to the narrower VibeProof ownership-verification direction.

Archived recommendations and claims may conflict with the active product. Use:

- [VibeProof documentation index](../../README.md)
- [VibeProof Product Decisions](../../decisions.md)

## Why the material was archived

The previous research tree mixed several product directions:

- KerjaProof as a broad serious-game assessment;
- Patch & Ship as an AI-generated pull-request incident;
- Unity or 3D as a possible primary experience;
- VibeProof as a focused artifact-ownership challenge;
- Miro wireframes for a homepage-latency scenario;
- hackathon-specific schedules and delivery assumptions.

The useful findings were consolidated into the active VibeProof product, assessment, research, and decision documents. The original sources remain here for provenance.
