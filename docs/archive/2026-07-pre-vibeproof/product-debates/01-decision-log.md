# Product Decision Log

## D001 - Brand the product VibeProof

Debate: `KerjaProof` communicated Malaysian identity and evidence, while `VibeProof` directly connected with the problem of vibe-coded work.

Decision: use `VibeProof` as the working brand.

Reason: it creates a memorable contrast between producing something that looks convincing and proving that the creator understands and can own it.

Tagline:

> Build with AI. Prove you know why it works.

## D002 - Do not detect AI use

Debate: employers may want to know whether a candidate vibe-coded a project.

Decision: allow AI and assess responsible use instead of attempting AI-code detection.

Reason: the final code does not reliably reveal who or what produced it. The employment-relevant question is whether the candidate can understand, verify, debug, adapt, and take responsibility for the result.

## D003 - Do not make 3D part of the engineering signal

Debate: a Unity environment could make the assessment memorable and immersive.

Decision: use a focused 2D engineering workspace for the software-engineering MVP. A decorative 3D shell is unnecessary.

Reason: software debugging happens through code, logs, tests, documentation, and deployment decisions. Movement or game familiarity would add irrelevant difficulty without improving evidence of engineering capability. Three-dimensional simulation may be appropriate later for spatial jobs such as field maintenance, warehouse operations, or emergency response.

## D004 - Do not make the product a text questionnaire

Debate: if the product is 2D, it could become a sequence of written questions.

Decision: require observable work actions inside an interactive incident workspace.

Required actions include inspecting artifacts, forming a hypothesis, running tests, using or challenging AI, responding to new evidence, making a deployment decision, and explaining the outcome.

Reason: verbal knowledge is not the same as demonstrated behavior.

## D005 - Do not claim to know how candidates think

Debate: the product could be described as tracking thinking or cognitive load.

Decision: describe the output as observable process evidence.

Reason: clicks, prompts, tests, decisions, explanations, and confidence ratings are behavior. They do not provide direct access to private thought, neural activity, or true cognitive load.

Approved language:

> VibeProof records evidence of how a candidate investigates, verifies, adapts, and explains work.

## D006 - Do not build another LeetCode

Debate: VibeProof could be explained as LeetCode with process tracking.

Decision: use that comparison only as a simple introduction, then clarify that VibeProof is an incident-based ownership challenge.

Reason: LeetCode primarily presents a defined algorithmic problem and checks the result. VibeProof presents incomplete evidence, an AI assistant, operational risk, changing constraints, and an obligation to justify a decision.

## D007 - Acknowledge Codility and CodeSignal as strong incumbents

Debate: process tracking, AI-assisted assessment, realistic tasks, and AI-generated reports initially appeared differentiating.

Decision: do not claim those features are unique.

Reason: Codility and CodeSignal already provide realistic IDEs, AI-assisted tasks, structured scoring, reports, simulations, and assessment authoring. Codility is particularly close to the original concept because it records AI interactions and includes tasks where common AI suggestions are deliberately wrong.

## D008 - Focus on proof of ownership

Debate: compete as a complete AI-native coding-assessment platform, build a Malaysian clone, or find a narrower verification problem.

Decision: focus on an artifact-grounded `Ownership Challenge`.

Reason: VibeProof can begin with a candidate's portfolio, pull request, take-home assignment, or controlled sample artifact, then ask the candidate to explain, break, adapt, verify, and defend it. This creates a clearer distinction from standardised task libraries.

## D009 - Complement existing tools instead of replacing them

Decision:

```text
Resume or ATS
  -> baseline assessment or take-home
  -> VibeProof Ownership Challenge
  -> human technical interview
```

Reason: a focused verification layer requires less buyer behavior change and avoids rebuilding the infrastructure of established assessment platforms.

## D010 - Improve the interview rather than replace it

Debate: an interviewer could simply ask the candidate questions verbally.

Decision: use VibeProof before the human interview to produce consistent work evidence and targeted follow-up questions.

Reason: a verbal answer shows what a candidate says they would do. A work simulation shows whether they inspect evidence, test assumptions, respond to failure, revise a hypothesis, and verify a change. Human judgment remains necessary for context, communication, and the final decision.
