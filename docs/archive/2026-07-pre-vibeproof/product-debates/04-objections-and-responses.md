# Product Objections and Responses

## Why not just ask the candidate verbally?

A verbal answer shows what the candidate says they would do. It may be correct, rehearsed, or generated with assistance.

VibeProof is useful only if the candidate must act: inspect evidence, run a test, respond to failure, revise a hypothesis, make a risk decision, and verify the outcome. The product should improve the later human interview by supplying evidence and targeted follow-up questions. It should not replace human judgment.

## Why not use Codility or CodeSignal?

Employers that need a complete and validated assessment platform may be better served by an incumbent.

VibeProof's proposed wedge is narrower: verify whether a candidate understands and can adapt a specific artifact they claim as their own. It can sit after a portfolio, take-home, or baseline assessment rather than replacing the existing platform.

## Is this just LeetCode with tracking?

No. That comparison is useful only as an introduction.

LeetCode usually gives a defined problem and evaluates the solution. VibeProof presents an incident with incomplete evidence, an AI assistant, changing constraints, operational risk, and a requirement to defend a decision tied to an artifact.

## How do we know that the candidate did not vibe-code?

We do not know reliably from the final code, and VibeProof should not claim that it does.

The candidate is allowed to use AI. VibeProof asks whether they can explain, test, challenge, adapt, and take responsibility for the output. Unverified AI dependence produces weak evidence; responsible AI-assisted engineering can produce strong evidence.

## Why not use a 3D world?

For software engineering, three-dimensional movement does not improve evidence about debugging or code ownership. It adds implementation cost, accessibility concerns, hardware risk, and possible gaming-experience bias.

Three-dimensional simulation should be reserved for roles where spatial context is part of the work, such as equipment maintenance, warehousing, safety, or emergency response.

## Is the product all text?

It is text-centred but not a text examination. The candidate uses an interactive workspace containing code, logs, test results, an AI assistant, incident updates, and deployment decisions. Visual status, timelines, and evidence panels make the experience operational rather than conversational.

## Can VibeProof measure cognitive load or know how candidates think?

Not directly. It can record behavioral indicators and collect self-reported workload for scenario research. It cannot infer private thoughts or neural activity from clicks and text.

The defensible output is observable process evidence: hypotheses, evidence inspected, tests selected, AI reliance, corrections, decisions, explanations, and confidence changes.

## Why would Malaysia need this?

Malaysia has growing interest in AI skills and workforce transformation, but need must be demonstrated with customer interviews. The strongest local hypothesis is that high-volume junior and graduate hiring teams need a shorter, more understandable ownership check than a full enterprise assessment platform.

Localisation, bilingual access, Malaysian scenarios, pay-per-use pricing, and local compliance can support adoption. They are not sufficient differentiation on their own.

## Why use Databricks?

Databricks can provide structured scenario generation, event storage, traceable analysis, and report generation. It should be the evidence and data layer, not a reason for the product to exist.

## Why use ElevenLabs?

Voice can narrate incident updates or transcribe a final explanation. It is an optional accessibility and presentation layer, not the assessment signal. A text alternative must always be available.

## Why not automatically reject low-scoring candidates?

The prototype is not sufficiently validated for automatic employment decisions. Adaptive tasks can vary in difficulty, automated analysis can be wrong, and hiring decisions have significant consequences. VibeProof should provide evidence and human follow-up recommendations.

## What happens if the candidate has no portfolio?

Offer a controlled sample artifact, such as the checkout incident used in the hackathon. The same ownership rubric can be applied without requiring prior project access.
