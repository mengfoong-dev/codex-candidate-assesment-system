01 the problem

Hiring engineers is broken
at both ends.

Ban AI in the interview

You measure a world that no longer exists. Nobody ships without an assistant anymore — so the score predicts a job that isn't the job.

✕ measures the past

Allow AI on the take-home

The classic grader tells you nothing. Anyone can paste a passing solution they can't explain, defend, or debug when it breaks.

✕ measures the paste

Both ends grade the answer. The answer is the one thing AI makes free.

02 the insight

The signal that predicts a good engineer isn't the fix.
It's the ownership.

Can they investigate an unfamiliar system, form and revise a hypothesis from evidence, use AI without being led off a cliff, and stand behind the fix? That's invisible to a pass/fail grader — and it's exactly what VibeProof records.

VibeProof measures the investigation, not the answer.

05 the idea that makes it trustworthy

Only auditable evidence earns points.
The AI only ever labels.
Behaviour is never scored.

It's called provenance separation, and it isn't a promise — it's enforced structurally. Every result carries an enum: deterministic · llm_rubric · context_index. The machine physically cannot score the thing it shouldn't.

10 how we built it

Agent-first, on a disciplined loop.

~39 tracked Codex (GPT-5.6) sessions over 3 days, each auto-timed by a lifecycle hook — so wall-clock is measured, not guessed. A design doc + TDD plan preceded every feature; the agent executed against an approved contract, never improvised.

Problem framing→ research validation→ Ownership Challenge→ observable evidence→ Proof Replay→ human interview

The signal for judges isn't prompt count — it's the chain. Decisions captured as ADRs & specs before execution, all under human review.

VibeProof
responsible-assessment boundary

The machine gathers the proof.
People make the call.

VibeProof records actions, prompts, tests and decisions — it does not read minds, does not claim to know who authored code, and does not predict job performance. It hands a human reviewer transparent, event-cited evidence. Final hiring decisions stay with people. (Decisions D006–D009.)
