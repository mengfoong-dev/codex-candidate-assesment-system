# VibeProof Product Brief

> Build with AI. Prove you know why it works.

## Product statement

VibeProof is a short, AI-allowed engineering Ownership Challenge that asks a candidate to understand, investigate, verify, adapt, and defend software work. It produces a chronological Proof Replay so a recruiter or technical interviewer can review the evidence behind the candidate's decisions.

VibeProof does not try to determine who typed each line of code. It asks whether the candidate can safely own the result.

## Problem

AI can help candidates create polished resumes, portfolio projects, take-home submissions, and working code. Those artifacts show what was produced, but they may provide weak evidence that the candidate can:

- explain how the software works;
- identify assumptions and failure modes;
- investigate production evidence;
- test AI-generated recommendations;
- adapt when new information appears;
- make and communicate a responsible operational decision.

Hiring teams with limited senior-engineer time need a short, job-relevant way to gather this evidence before a full technical interview.

## Target users

### Initial buyer

Malaysian recruitment agencies, graduate programmes, startups, and mid-sized technical employers hiring junior or intermediate software engineers.

The best initial segment has meaningful application volume, constrained interviewer time, and an existing portfolio, take-home, or baseline-assessment step.

### Candidate

A software-engineering applicant who may use modern AI tools and needs a fair opportunity to demonstrate understanding, verification, and ownership.

### Human reviewer

A recruiter, hiring manager, or technical interviewer who needs understandable evidence and targeted follow-up questions rather than an unexplained score.

## Position in the hiring journey

```text
Application or ATS
  -> portfolio, take-home, or baseline assessment
  -> VibeProof Ownership Challenge
  -> Proof Replay
  -> targeted human technical interview
```

VibeProof is a verification layer. It does not replace an applicant-tracking system, a complete coding-assessment platform, or human judgment.

## Value proposition

### For hiring teams

- Observe job-relevant investigation and verification before spending senior interview time.
- Review the evidence behind a conclusion instead of relying on a single score.
- Ask better interview questions based on the candidate's actual actions.
- Allow responsible AI use instead of creating an unrealistic AI-free exercise.

### For candidates

- Demonstrate practical engineering judgment beyond algorithm speed or resume claims.
- Use AI openly while showing how suggestions are checked and improved.
- Receive an assessment based on a defined incident and transparent rubric.
- Explain uncertainty, trade-offs, and operational risk.

## Competitive wedge

Codility and CodeSignal already provide strong technical-assessment platforms, realistic development environments, AI-assisted tasks, reports, and configurable scoring. VibeProof should not claim those capabilities are unique.

The proposed wedge is narrower:

- verify ownership of a candidate-claimed or controlled software artifact;
- use three proof moments: explain, break, and adapt with verification;
- produce a chronological Proof Replay tied to an ownership claim;
- fit between an existing submission and the human interview;
- start with a short, understandable workflow for smaller hiring teams.

This market position remains a hypothesis until customer interviews and pilots demonstrate demand.

## Non-goals

The MVP will not:

- attribute code authorship or determine whether AI generated it;
- identify cheating from writing style or source code;
- replace a full technical interview;
- automatically make an employment decision;
- become a full ATS or enterprise assessment platform;
- support arbitrary repository execution;
- infer intelligence, personality, neural activity, or private thoughts;
- make 3D navigation part of the engineering score.

## Responsible positioning

Approved description:

> VibeProof records evidence of how a candidate investigates, verifies, adapts, and explains software work during a controlled AI-assisted incident.

The product is a research-informed prototype, not a validated psychometric hiring test. Reliability, fairness, accessibility, job relevance, and reviewer agreement must be studied before high-stakes deployment. Final hiring decisions remain with people.
