# Pitch Language and Responsible Claims

## 30-second pitch

> AI has made it easy to produce code that looks impressive. But production engineering is not only about generating code. It is about understanding requirements, verifying AI output, debugging failures, testing edge cases, and taking responsibility for what gets shipped. KerjaProof places candidates in a realistic AI-assisted incident and gives employers transparent evidence of their engineering ownership.

## Why choose us?

> KerjaProof does not punish candidates for using AI. It evaluates whether they can responsibly direct, verify, debug, test, and own AI-assisted code.

## Why now?

> The hiring signal is changing. Résumés and take-home code can both be heavily optimized with AI. Employers need a short, job-relevant way to observe how candidates work with modern tools.

## What makes us different?

- We measure engineering behavior, not résumé keywords.
- We allow AI instead of trying to detect it.
- We test verification and ownership, not just code generation.
- We show evidence behind the score.
- We are designed for Malaysian SMEs and bilingual access.
- We support human review rather than automatic rejection.

## Scientific claim

Use:

> KerjaProof is a research-informed serious-game prototype that observes job-relevant behavior associated with executive control, metacognition, error monitoring, and responsible AI reliance.

Do not use:

> KerjaProof knows what candidates are thinking.

Do not use:

> KerjaProof accurately predicts future job performance.

Do not use:

> KerjaProof detects whether candidates cheated with AI.

## Responsible-AI statement

> KerjaProof does not make an automatic hiring or rejection decision. It gives employers structured evidence from a controlled work simulation so a human reviewer can make a better-informed decision.

## Future validation roadmap

1. Pilot the assessment with software engineers of different experience levels.
2. Have independent senior engineers rate the same sessions.
3. Compare automated scores with expert ratings.
4. Test repeatability across equivalent scenarios.
5. Test fairness across language, device, accessibility, and gaming-experience groups.
6. Eventually compare assessment results with real onboarding and job outcomes.
