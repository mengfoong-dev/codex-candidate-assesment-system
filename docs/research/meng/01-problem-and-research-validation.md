# Problem and Research Validation

## Problem statement

Generative AI has made it easier for candidates to optimize résumés, match job-description keywords, generate professional descriptions, and produce code. As a result, documents and submitted artifacts may no longer provide enough evidence of what a candidate can independently understand, verify, debug, and maintain.

For software roles, the relevant shift is not that candidates use AI. Modern developers should use AI. The problem is that candidates may submit AI-generated code without understanding the requirements, checking edge cases, testing the output, or taking responsibility for what is shipped.

## User problem

Malaysian SMEs and startups may not have enough senior engineering capacity to evaluate every candidate deeply. Hiring teams need a short, realistic, and transparent way to observe engineering judgment before investing in interviews.

## Research evidence from the supplied PDF

The supplied document, [Research Validation & Industry Evidence](Research%20Validation%20%26%20Industry%20Evidence%20%281%29.pdf), supports the following direction:

- Work-sample assessments are relevant because candidates perform tasks that resemble real work.
- Game-based assessments can measure job-relevant cognitive and behavioral signals when designed around a defined competency.
- Behavioral performance may provide information that self-reported confidence or résumé claims do not.
- Existing market examples show demand for task-based or simulation-based assessment.

## How to use the evidence responsibly

The research supports the assessment approach, not the claim that this prototype is already scientifically validated.

Use:

> Research suggests that job-relevant work samples and structured simulations can provide useful evidence beyond self-report and résumé claims.

Avoid:

> Our game can accurately predict who will succeed at work.

That stronger claim would require role-specific validation, reliability studies, criterion-related validation against real job outcomes, and fairness testing.

## Hypothesis

> In a controlled, job-relevant software incident, observable decisions about AI-generated code, testing, debugging, risk, and adaptation will provide more useful early evidence of engineering capability than résumé claims or an unverified take-home submission.

## Validation plan after the hackathon

The next research phase should:

1. Define the engineering competencies through job analysis and interviews with experienced developers.
2. Create multiple equivalent scenarios to reduce memorization effects.
3. Ask experienced engineers to independently rate candidate sessions.
4. Compare game scores with expert ratings and later work outcomes.
5. Test reliability across repeated sessions.
6. Audit whether language, gaming experience, device, or accessibility factors affect results.
