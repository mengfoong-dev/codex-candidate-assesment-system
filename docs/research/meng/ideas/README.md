# VibeProof Ideas and Debate Record

This folder records the product debates, objections, strategic decisions, and assumptions behind VibeProof. It is intended to preserve not only what the team decided, but why the decision was made.

## Current direction as of 14 July 2026

> VibeProof is a short, artifact-grounded ownership challenge for AI-assisted engineering. A candidate must explain, debug, adapt, test, and take responsibility for work they claim as their own.

The current positioning is:

> Build with AI. Prove you know why it works.

The recommended market position is a verification layer between a candidate submission and a human technical interview, rather than a replacement for Codility, CodeSignal, or the interview itself.

## Settled decisions

- Use the product name `VibeProof`.
- Do not try to detect whether code was written by AI.
- Evaluate understanding, verification, adaptation, and ownership.
- Use a focused 2D engineering workspace for the software-engineering assessment.
- Do not claim to read thoughts, measure brain activity, or directly measure cognitive load.
- Do not build another generic LeetCode, Codility, or CodeSignal clone.
- Start from a candidate-claimed artifact or a controlled sample artifact.
- Produce an observable evidence replay for a human reviewer.
- Keep final hiring decisions with people.
