HackerRank

Bucket A · Dev assessment · Incumbent

HackerRank

Threat to us B+

The default technical screen: a full-funnel platform (Screen → Interview → Chakra AI interviewer) that wins on brand, the biggest question bank, and the deepest ATS distribution — not on signal quality.

BBiz modelC+USPA–FeaturesBArchitectureDReal-work
5 product surfaces77 roles · 260+ skills · 7,000+ Qs40+ ATS integrations
How wide & how deep it goes
Breadth

Five surfaces: Screen (async auto-scored assessments), Interview (live shared-IDE with real Git repos + whiteboard + playback), Chakra (autonomous AI voice/video interviewer, ex-YC), Engage (hackathons/sourcing), and a 26M+ developer community. 77 roles, 260+ skills, junior-to-staff, pre-built Certified Assessments + JD-generated custom tests.

Depth

Question types span auto-graded coding (40+ langs), MCQ, SQL, front-end, and Projects (real full-stack repos). Results are per-skill scorecards, not one number. A deliberate AI-era layer separately measures fundamentals, AI-fluency, and AI concepts (RAG, prompts). Chakra ingests a JD, adapts follow-ups live, and scores 4 dimensions on one fixed rubric. Integrity stack cut plagiarism false-positives from 10%→4% across 35k applicants.

How screening works (end to end)
  1. Configure the roleRecruiter

    Pick a pre-built Certified Assessment, assemble a custom test, or upload a JD to auto-generate topics/questions. Set time limit, languages, AI-use policy.

  2. Calibrate & test-driveRecruiter

    Tune difficulty; for Chakra, take the interview yourself to sanity-check depth before it goes live.

  3. Invite from the ATSRecruiter

    One-click send from Greenhouse/Lever/Workday; single-link email, auto-fireable on stage-change.

  4. Candidate completes asyncCandidate

    Either a timed 60–90 min coding test or a 20–30 min Chakra live adaptive interview — no scheduling.

  5. AI administers & adaptsSystem·AI

    Coding auto-graded on hidden test cases; Chakra probes shallow answers, escalates difficulty, drops into an embedded IDE.

  6. Live integrity monitoringSystem·AI

    Identity/multi-face checks, tab/copy-paste/multi-monitor tracking, AI proctoring; full session replay captured.

  7. Auto-score & reportSystem·AI

    Evidence report in minutes: overall + per-skill grades, rationale tied to transcript moments, integrity flags, recording.

  8. Ranked results into the ATSSystem·AI

    Pre-scored, ranked candidates + scorecards appear alongside the profile — no manual transfer.

  9. Recruiter reviews shortlistRecruiter

    Team reads evidence only for candidates who cleared the bar; weak/flagged ones filtered before human time.

  10. Live technical interviewRecruiter

    Shared IDE + real repos + whiteboard, with a Screen-to-Interview facial match to confirm same person; Scorecard Assist auto-fills the rubric.

  11. Hiring decisionRecruiter

    Compare standardized scorecards across finalists; funnel/pass-rate analytics feed back into role calibration.

CodeSignal

Bucket A · Dev assessment · AI-native #2

CodeSignal

Threat to us A–

The most philosophically dangerous rival: it already scores how well you work with AI (Cosmo copilot), conceding the open-book future our product is built for — and it has the strongest scoring science in the set.

BBiz modelA–USPA–FeaturesB+ArchitectureD+Real-work
200–600 standardized scoreCosmo (GPT-4o) copilot~2,800 hrs validation
How wide & how deep it goes
Breadth

Certified assessments (General Coding, Industry Coding, Data, Front-End, ML, System Design, QA) + business/GTM simulations + a live pair-programming Interview IDE + an AI Interviewer suite (assessments, AI phone screens, video avatars) + Cosmo (GPT-4o) + a Learn product. ATS connectors + SSO/API + Executive Analytics.

Depth

Deepest scoring science here: the 200–600 score uses a two-tier model (base + difficulty-weighted completion bonus, IRT-style). GCA = 4 questions scored on correctness/speed/implementation/problem-solving, 628k+ variations, 45 languages. Certified tests carry ~2,800 hrs research + adverse-impact studies for EEOC/OFCCP. Cosmo is role-aware, never gives the answer, and logs a full “AI Assistance Transcript.” Suspicion Score fuses paste/similarity/copy/language-switch signals.

How screening works (end to end)
  1. Pick / build assessmentRecruiter

    Choose a certified assessment or build custom (question slots, point weights, allowed languages, randomization); Cosmo can auto-assemble.

  2. Set the cut scoreRecruiter

    5-step guide: confirm role fit (JD review or 45-min incumbent focus group), define “minimally acceptable,” benchmark vs pass-rate data, pick a threshold (respect the 80% adverse-impact rule).

  3. Invite candidatesRecruiter

    Email, shareable link, or ATS push; candidates may submit an existing valid score to skip re-testing.

  4. Identity + environment checkCandidate

    If proctored: government ID + webcam selfie + screen/cam/mic recording consent.

  5. Take the assessmentCandidate

    Timed (GCA = 70 min, 4 Qs) in the browser IDE; run/debug against test cases. Cosmo Chat available for help (not answers), fully logged.

  6. Auto-scoringSystem·AI

    Two-tier engine computes the 200–600 score + skill-area proficiencies instantly.

  7. Integrity analysisSystem·AI

    Suspicion Score (paste/similarity vs global corpus/copy/language-switch) → None–High + Integrity-Flagged verdict.

  8. Human proctoring reviewSystem·AI

    Specialists review ID + recording; certified or rejected within 1–3 business days.

  9. Review reports & replaysRecruiter

    Coding Report: overall + per-question scores, keystroke replay, AI-assistance transcript, Investigate view; compare to cut score.

  10. Live or AI interviewRecruiter

    Advance to a live pair-IDE interview or an AI Interviewer / phone screen / video avatar that scores against a rubric.

  11. Calibrate & decideRecruiter

    Decide on the holistic score; over time recalibrate the cut score against interview + on-the-job outcomes (optional validation study).

Eightfold AI

Bucket C · Talent intelligence · Category leader

Eightfold AI

Threat to us C+

The enterprise talent-intelligence ceiling: a full-lifecycle platform on a deep-learning Talent Graph that infers skills and never verifies them — making it a likely partner/acquirer more than a head-to-head rival.

B+Biz modelA–USPAFeaturesAArchitectureFReal-work
Talent Graph · ~1.6B profilesAI Interviewer · 22+ languagesBest fit 5,000–10,000+ employees
How wide & how deep it goes
Breadth

An enterprise operating system across the whole talent lifecycle: Talent Acquisition, Talent Management, Marketplace/Internal Mobility, Workforce Exchange, Talent Design/Forge (skills taxonomy + low-code apps), and Resource Management. AI agents: AI Interviewer, real-time Interview Companion, and 360 Interview (screen + functional + coding + language in one). Covers trades to engineering to corporate.

Depth

The Talent Graph (~1.6B profiles / 1.6M skills — treat as marketing) infers skills a candidate never listed and scores “learnability,” surfacing non-obvious matches with an explainability panel. AI Interviewer adapts questions from resume + JD, using STAR / coding-reasoning / CEFR rubrics, and deliberately scores answer content only — not face, tone, or accent (bias control). Deep on inference/matching + scale; shallower than a specialist on any single deep coding test.

How screening works (end to end)
  1. Define / calibrate the roleRecruiter

    Pick a role template; the skills vocabulary auto-expands via the Talent Graph to carry inferred adjacent/learnable skills.

  2. Source & rediscoverSystem·AI

    AI scans the whole Talent Network + your own past applicants, matching on skills + potential, not keywords.

  3. Match, score & rankSystem·AI

    Deep-learning ranks every candidate with a why-recommended explainability view.

  4. Recruiter shortlistsRecruiter

    Human reviews ranked list + rationale and decides who advances.

  5. Invite to AI interviewSystem·AI

    On-demand invite, 24/7, 22+ languages, often triggered right after application.

  6. Candidate self-servesCandidate

    System/mic/cam check + recording consent (+ ID if required), then an adaptive conversation from resume + JD.

  7. AI evaluates & scoresSystem·AI

    Same rubric for all (STAR / reasoning / CEFR), answer-content only; 360 Interview can fold multiple rounds into one.

  8. Structured summary deliveredSystem·AI

    Within hours: a rubric-aligned intelligence summary per candidate, consistent across the pool.

  9. Human interview + CompanionRecruiter

    Companion joins Zoom/Meet/Teams, transcribes, tracks competency coverage, pre-fills the scorecard.

  10. Decision & offerRecruiter

    Compare consistently-scored candidates; source→interview→offer on one platform (or pushed to the ATS).

~5× recruiter throughputAuto match/rank + autonomous interviews (case: 160+ hrs saved / 2 mo).

Consistent, defensibleSame rubric, answer-content-only scoring, auditable records.
Internal mobility / retentionSame graph redeploys existing staff — the buy-vs-borrow lever.

What you actually receive: match ranking + explainability, a rubric-aligned intelligence summary per candidate, human-interview transcript + competency tracking + pre-filled scorecard, a skills-based role library + workforce skills inventory + gap analysis, internal-mobility recommendations, and pipeline/skills/diversity dashboards writing back to the ATS/HRIS. Note: reviewers flag analytics & UI as relative weak points.
Onboarding & time-to-valueAn enterprise implementation, not self-serve: skills config + data calibration + ATS/HRIS wiring + interview design; needs a TA-ops team. ~3–6 months (newer tooling → ~10–12 wks); 6–9 months to real productivity.

Mercor

Bucket B · AI-recruiter marketplace · Hypergrowth

Mercor

Threat to us A–

The closest to us architecturally — it actually crawls GitHub — and the highest-potential player by growth. But it reads presence, not executed code, and its trust cracked after a major 2026 breach.

C+Biz modelA–USPB+FeaturesB+ArchitectureCReal-work
Two-sided marketplace · 300k+ profiles~10k AI interviews/day~$2B run-rate (reported)
How wide & how deep it goes
Breadth

Seven surfaces: sourcing (crawls resume/GitHub/LinkedIn/portfolio/Twitter), AI video interview (“Monty,” ~10k/day), profile synthesis (0–100 Match Score), semantic search + ranked matching, contracting (IP/ToW/offers), payments (weekly Stripe/Wise + Insightful time-tracking + tax forms), and APEX evaluation benchmarks. Two buyers: frontier AI labs (RLHF experts) + enterprises hiring engineers/professionals.

Depth

GitHub crawl extracts repos/commits/languages and LLM-ranks project complexity. The AI interview generates questions from resume + JD and listens for specificity, ownership language, communication efficiency, and technical depth, plus proctoring. Output is a 0–100 Match Score + leaderboard. Mercor holds patents on forecasting retention/success from interview + placement history, and credentials domain experts (MD/JD/PhD) for AI-training task types.

How screening works (end to end)
  1. Profile + resume submitCandidate

    Register, upload resume, link GitHub/LinkedIn/portfolio; crawlers pull public data immediately.

  2. Automated enrichmentSystem·AI

    Extract work history, employer signals, GitHub complexity; build + index a composite profile before any interview.

  3. Browse & applyCandidate

    Apply to roles or receive inbound matches; a non-scoring practice interview is available.

  4. AI video interview (Monty)Candidate

    15–20 min live conversation, questions from resume + JD, real-time adaptive; camera-on + gov-ID; up to 3 retakes.

  5. AI scoring + transcriptSystem·AI

    Auto-transcript + scores on specificity/ownership/efficiency/technical depth + proctoring; 0–100 Match Score.

  6. Index + shortlist genSystem·AI

    Profile indexed into semantic search; a natural-language brief returns a ranked shortlist in <24 hrs.

  7. Client reviews leaderboardClient

    Ranked cards + interview clips + AI eval notes, side-by-side — no sourcing/first-screen work.

  8. Client selects + offerClient

    One-click select; direct hire or project contract; optional client-run follow-up interview.

  9. ID verify + background checkSystem·AI

    Persona ID + background check (1–2 wks); no hours logged until cleared.

  10. Contracting + complianceSystem·AI

    Sign ToW, IP/CIAA, and tax forms (W-9/W-8BEN) in-platform; Okta SSO.

  11. Time-tracking + weekly payCandidate

    Insightful monitoring for hourly work; weekly payouts via Stripe/Wise, no client invoicing.

The through-line

Verdict

Sophisticated pipelines, all built on a signal they can’t fully trust.

Every one of these runs a deep, polished screening funnel — but HackerRank and CodeSignal test synthetic puzzles, Eightfold infers from history, and Mercor reads GitHub presence. None executes a candidate’s actual code or verifies authorship, which is exactly why their integrity, proctoring, and false-positive problems never fully close. Out-position CodeSignal on philosophy, out-execute Mercor on depth, partner toward Eightfold, and beat HackerRank before its distribution catches up — all from the same wedge: verify the work, not the test.
